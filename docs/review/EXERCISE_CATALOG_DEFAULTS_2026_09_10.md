## Guardrails

- Do not modify completed session snapshots.
- Do not silently overwrite explicit planned-workout fields or intervals; defaults apply when adding new movements unless provenance says they were inherited.
- Do not regex-migrate every occurrence of plank/hold/dip/sled or every Core/Mobility/Jumping category.
- Keep load optional for bodyweight/bands; loaded variants and athlete intent remain valid.
- These are measurement metadata recommendations, not exercise technique, medical or training prescriptions.

RPE inclusion was preserved in this inventory to isolate semantic errors; a separate UI decision can make effort optional. Exact rationale and IDs are in catalog-findings.json.
